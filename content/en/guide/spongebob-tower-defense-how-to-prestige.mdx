## What Prestige Does in SpongeBob Tower Defense (and What You Keep)

Prestige is the game's soft reset. You trade your current level and run progress for Prestige Tokens, which are spent at the Prestige Shop on permanent upgrades. In practice, that means every prestige makes your next climb smoother: more starting power, a better economy, and a shorter road back to Level 100.

The catch is that most of your visible progress disappears the moment you confirm. Players who look up SpongeBob Tower Defense how to prestige usually do it because they've stalled — they can't push deeper waves, their income has plateaued, and the only meaningful power left sits behind the Prestige Shop. That's the signal you're ready.

| Outcome | What Happens | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Player level | Resets to the beginning | You re-earn progression from scratch |
| Prestige Tokens | Awarded when you prestige | Permanent currency you never lose |
| Prestige Shop | Unlocked and stocked | Source of long-term upgrades |
| Unit roster | Community reports say the cave route preserves your units | Saves hours of rebuilding |
| Wave progress | Starts over | Expect a slower first few waves |

One important note: community reports describe a cave-based prestige that lets you keep every unit instead of starting from an empty roster. Treat that as player experience rather than a guarantee — SpongeBob Tower Defense gets updated regularly, and small changes can shift how the reset behaves. Always confirm the current rules in-game before you commit.

## Prestige Requirements: Level 100 and the Hidden Kelp Cave

There is exactly one hard requirement: you must be Level 100. Until you hit that number, the prestige interaction simply won't be available to you, no matter how many tokens you think you've earned.

The location is the part that trips people up. To the right of spawn, in front of SpongeBob's Pineapple, there's a slightly hidden cave entrance surrounded by kelp. It doesn't glow like a quest marker, and it doesn't sit on the main path, which is why so many players hit Level 100 and then wander around asking how to prestige at all. Walk past the pineapple, hug the right side of the map, and look for the opening in the vegetation.

| Location | Kelp cave right of spawn, in front of the pineapple | Slightly hidden; check the right side |

## SpongeBob Tower Defense How to Prestige: Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Once you know the location, the actual process takes under a minute. Here's the full SpongeBob Tower Defense how to prestige sequence from a fresh session.

1. **Grind to Level 100.** Play normally and track your level on the HUD. Don't prestige early — the option won't appear until you hit the cap.
2. **Finish or safely exit your current match.** You don't want to lose a run you were mid-way through.
3. **Return to the spawn area.** The pineapple is your landmark.
4. **Move right along the edge of the map.** The kelp cave sits in front of the pineapple, tucked slightly out of view.
5. **Enter the cave.** This is the Prestige Cave referenced in community guides.
6. **Interact with the prestige prompt.** Read the confirmation carefully — this is the point of no return.
7. **Confirm the prestige.** Your level resets and tokens are awarded.
8. **Visit the Prestige Shop.** Spend immediately on a permanent upgrade so your next run starts stronger.

### Mistakes That Waste Your First Prestige

- **Prestiging the second you hit 100 without a plan.** Know what you're buying before you reset.
- **Ignoring the shop afterward.** Tokens sitting unspent are the same as no tokens at all.
- **Assuming your loadout carries over.** Community reports suggest the cave route keeps units, but verify it yourself.
- **Prestiging during a limited-time event.** You'll lose the level progress you need for event rewards.

## Prestige Tokens and the Prestige Shop: Spending Priorities

Prestige Tokens are the whole point of the reset. They're a separate currency from anything you earn in a normal match, and they're only usable at the Prestige Shop inside the prestige area. Because they're permanent, the order you spend them in shapes your account for dozens of hours.

The general rule from experienced players: buy whatever makes leveling faster before you buy anything that makes you stronger at the top end. A permanent income or progression boost compounds every single run, while a flat damage bump only helps once you're already deep.

| Priority | Upgrade Type | Why It Comes First | Skip If |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Progression and XP boosts | Shortens every future climb to 100 | You only play casually |
| 2 | Economy and cash upgrades | Funds stronger towers earlier in each run | Your income already feels fine |
| 3 | Global damage or buffs | Pushes you into higher waves | You can't reach the waves where it matters |
| 4 | Utility and quality-of-life | Smooths out awkward early waves | You're saving for a bigger unlock |
| 5 | Cosmetics and flex items | Pure preference | You still need functional power |

If you're unsure, buy the cheapest permanent upgrade first. It's a small commitment, it pays off immediately, and it teaches you how much a single token tier actually changes your runs.

## FAQ

**What level do you need to be to prestige in SpongeBob Tower Defense?**
You need to reach Level 100. Until then, the prestige interaction in the kelp cave won't trigger, regardless of how much progress you've made elsewhere.

**Do you lose your units when you prestige?**
Community reports say that entering the kelp cave near the pineapple lets you prestige while keeping your units, rather than wiping your roster. Because the game is updated often, confirm the current behavior in-game before you reset.

**Where exactly is the prestige cave?**
To the right of spawn, in front of SpongeBob's Pineapple. It's a slightly hidden kelp-covered entrance, which is why many players hit Level 100 and still can't find it.

**What should I buy first with Prestige Tokens?**
Start with permanent progression or economy upgrades. They speed up every future climb, which means you'll earn the next batch of tokens faster. Damage and cosmetic upgrades come later.
