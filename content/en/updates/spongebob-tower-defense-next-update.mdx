## How to Prepare Before the Next Update Drops

Preparation is the difference between finishing the new chapters in a weekend and grinding them for a month. Here is a practical checklist.

| Priority | Task | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| High | Bank gems and stop spending on rerolls | New units and upgrades almost always cost premium currency |
| High | Finish any unfinished story chapters you can | Keeps your progression path clear for the new world |
| Medium | Stock boosters and consumables | Speeds up event grinding during the opening window |
| Medium | Level your core damage towers | Endless modes punish shallow rosters |
| Low | Screenshot your current codes | Community code lists change often, and expired ones waste time |

### A Three-Step Prep Plan

1. **Lock your gem spending.** Decide on a personal gem target and treat it as untouchable until the patch lands. The temptation to reroll for a shiny unit is real, and it is the single most common way players enter a new update broke.
2. **Audit your roster.** Identify your two or three strongest damage towers and your best support units. Everything else is noise until the new content is cleared.
3. **Clear your backlog.** Any old chapters or side objectives you have been ignoring are worth finishing now, because a new world tends to push old content out of the spotlight — and out of the matchmaking pool of players willing to help.

If you only do one thing, do the first one. Gems are the bottleneck for nearly every player heading into a major release.
